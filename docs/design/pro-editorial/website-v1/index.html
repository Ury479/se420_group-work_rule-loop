A few things to know

Clarity, before
commitment.

What can I use today?

The interactive example runs locally in your browser. Accounts, saved loops, AI reviews and paid plans are not available yet.

Do I need to journal every day?

No. Start with one decision worth revisiting, such as a subscription renewal. Return when you have something useful to compare.

Is the example powered by AI?

No. It arranges your inputs into a four-part preview. Nothing you type in the example is sent to a model or stored on our server.

What happens after I join?

Your email and plan preference are saved for early-access updates. Joining does not reserve a price or start a subscription.
