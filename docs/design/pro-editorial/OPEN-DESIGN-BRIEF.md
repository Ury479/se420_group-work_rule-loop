# RuleLoop · The art of choosing well

Status: Design brief, 2026-09-28. Deliver isolated high-fidelity concepts, not production routes.

## Product
RuleLoop helps people make better decisions about AI tools/subscriptions, use what they buy, and turn a useful lesson into one action for the next similar situation. Two chosen directions: subscription value reviews + contextual behavior reviews. The shared loop is Expectation → Evidence → Unknowns → One next experiment. Never claim AI knows actual outcomes without user confirmation. No fabricated savings, testimonials, customers, integrations, live AI, or payment success.

## Visual direction
Hermès-inspired artisanal editorial luxury, interpreted for a useful digital product. Warm ivory paper, burnt orange accents, ink brown, muted terracotta. Generous deliberate whitespace, unusually beautiful serif typography, quiet readable sans-serif text, precise rules and asymmetrical editorial composition. An original sculptural orange ribbon/loop and paper decision-card motif can give the page its signature. No Hermès logo, product photos, trademark imitation, fake affiliation, purple gradients, dashboard cliché, glitter, piles of rounded cards, or emoji icons. The attached luxury system is a starting point; this user brief overrides conflicting palette choices.

Apply the grid/type/rhythm/quality methods of /Applications/Open Design.app/Contents/Resources/open-design/design-templates/guizang-ppt/SKILL.md and its references/checklist.md. This is a vertical responsive website, NOT a horizontal slide deck. Apply frontend-design skill. No need for extra discovery questions: user chose direction and scope.

## Homepage composition
Navigation: RuleLoop wordmark, The method, Try a loop, Pricing, Start free. Mobile menu must work.
Hero: small eyebrow 'A little more intention.'; headline 'Make room for better decisions.'; body 'Know what your tools are doing for you. Turn what you learn into a better next move.' Primary CTA 'Try a decision', secondary 'Explore Pro'. Include a beautiful distinctive original hero composition (vector/CSS illustration is appropriate) and visible short product-card evidence without misleading claims.
A real interactive sampler prominent within the first two screens, anchor #try: preset sample about renewing a $20 AI tool. Label 'Interactive example · no account needed'. Allow editing tool/name, expectation and observed result. Button 'Build my preview' creates a deterministic local four-part card from entered text; label clearly 'Preview, not an AI analysis'. Missing evidence stays unknown. Do not infer a verdict. No persistence claim; session memory only. Reset example control. 'Keep this loop' opens an honest account-required/design-preview message. Provide inline validation, keyboard focus, accessible status.
Feature editorial sections: 'Remember the reason'; 'See what actually changed'; 'Carry one lesson forward'. Show realistic small visual evidence/expectation comparison and a rule such as 'Before adding another tool, finish one real task with what I have.' This is a proposed rule, not a proven success.
Explain the four-step method with numbered typographic rhythm; keep copy short.
A compact Free / Pro price invitation.
A strong spacious CTA near end: 'Your next decision can be a little clearer.'
FAQ using native accessible details: what RuleLoop does, whether daily journaling is required (no), how AI uses selected evidence (candidate analysis, user confirms), whether the preview is real AI (no), Pro allowance, billing/cancellation.
Footer: brand, small purpose statement, Method / Try / Pricing links. Privacy and Terms must open clearly labeled policy-preview dialogs, not broken links or falsely claim final legal policy. Include discreet 'Design preview — AI, accounts and payments are not connected.'

## Pricing contract for this concept
User wording interpreted as Pro USD $5/month or USD $60/year (awaiting clarification; no discount). Design both billing states.
Monthly selected initially: '$5 / month', '$5 billed monthly'. Annual: '$60 / year', 'Equivalent to $5/month. $60 billed annually.' No 'save', no fake strikethrough, no 'most popular' unsupported badge. Both auto-renew at selected interval in proposed product; clear renewal/cancel copy in FAQ, no refund promise. Tax if applicable shown before payment.
Free proposed package: 1 active saved loop, 1 AI review per month, manual reflections, history/export. Homepage interactive example does not consume AI quota and does not require signup.
Pro proposed package: up to 20 active loops, 20 AI reviews per month, cross-loop pattern suggestions, checkpoint reminders, history/export. Both monthly and annual plans receive the SAME monthly 20-review allowance, no rollover. 'AI review' means one successful generated analysis; failure/retry of same job is not billed twice. These quotas are proposed for design and documented as assumptions, not claimed as already shipped.
Use two tasteful Free and Pro blocks, comparison rows, an annual/monthly accessible toggle, a short 'What counts as a review?' section and billing FAQ. Feature list must distinguish shared manual/export basics from Pro analysis. At quota limit manual tools remain usable, existing history never locked. 'Choose Pro' opens a clearly labeled demo order summary with selected interval, amount, quota, 'Checkout is not connected in this design preview'; never invent successful checkout or ask for a real card.
